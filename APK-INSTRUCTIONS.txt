UPLOAD: Upload all files and folders from this ZIP to the root of your OromoSTEM GitHub repository. Commit. Wait for successful GitHub Pages deployment.
BUILD: Open https://www.pwabuilder.com/ and enter https://abdi-gebisa.github.io/OromoSTEM/ . Select Package for stores > Android > Other Android > Generate/Download Package. Select a NEW signing key (not unsigned) if available and keep signing files private.
IMPORTANT: This ZIP is NOT an APK. PWABuilder produces the APK. It may require /.well-known/assetlinks.json at the DOMAIN ROOT (https://abdi-gebisa.github.io/.well-known/assetlinks.json), not inside OromoSTEM. This may require updating the separate abdi-gebisa.github.io root repository. Without it Android may show the browser address bar. Test on your phone before sharing.
