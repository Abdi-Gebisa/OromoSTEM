Field notes · Core ideas

Keep these relationships beside you while experimenting.

01 · Projectile motion

vₓ = v₀ cos θ
vᵧ = v₀ sin θ − gt
x = v₀ cos θ · t

Ignoring air resistance, horizontal acceleration is zero and vertical acceleration is −g. The horizontal and vertical motions share the same time.

02 · Snell's law

n₁ sin θ₁ = n₂ sin θ₂

The refractive index describes how light propagates in a medium. Angles are measured from the normal. Entering a higher-index medium bends a ray toward the normal.

03 · Total internal reflection

sin θc = n₂/n₁, where n₁ > n₂

Total internal reflection is possible only when light travels from a higher refractive index to a lower one and the incident angle exceeds the critical angle.

04 · Thin lenses

1/f = 1/dₒ + 1/dᵢ
m = −dᵢ/dₒ

For a converging lens, a positive image distance indicates a real image; a negative distance indicates a virtual image. The sign of magnification indicates orientation.

A note on scientific models

These are idealized teaching simulations. The projectile model neglects air resistance; the lens is thin and ideal; the optics interface is flat. Real experiments may differ because of measurement uncertainty, material properties and equipment limitations.
